## Contingency Plans

### Scenario 1: Low Turnout (< 20 people show up)
**Action:**
- Proceed with event as planned (intimate setting = more 1:1 support)
- Community Host adjusts icebreaker to fit smaller group
- Use time for deeper Q&A, personalized troubleshooting
- Post-event: Analyze registration-to-attendance drop-off, improve reminders for Week 2

### Scenario 2: Technical Failure (WiFi outage, projector failure)
**Action:**
- **WiFi down:** Switch to offline activities (pre-downloaded Unity/Godot projects on USB drives)
- **Projector fails:** Facilitator uses whiteboard to sketch concepts, shares screen via participant laptops
- **Livestream fails:** Apologize to online folks, record session, share later
- Always have backup: mobile hotspot, spare HDMI cables, USB with all materials

### Scenario 3: Overwhelming Skill Gaps (some participants way ahead, some stuck)
**Action:**
- Pair advanced participants with beginners (peer mentorship)
- Floater provides 1:1 support to those struggling
- Facilitator maintains pace for majority, shares "stretch challenges" for fast learners
- Post-event: Create skill-based breakout groups for Week 2+

### Scenario 4: Disruptive Participant
**Action:**
- Community Host privately approaches participant to address behavior
- If persistent, Event Lead intervenes (reminder of community guidelines)
- Last resort: politely ask participant to leave, offer 1:1 follow-up later
- Prioritize safety and inclusivity of group

### Scenario 5: Time Overrun
**Action:**
- Event Lead monitors time, signals to facilitator at -10 min mark
- If behind: skip "Show & Tell" (share videos in Discord later), prioritize networking
- If way behind: extend by 15 min (announce to participants), or schedule a follow-up "Office Hours" session
