# CONTENT_OPS_PLAN.md
**Content Operations Plan: 4-Week Realm Starter Series**  
Digital Realm Entertainment  


## Overview

**Campaign Name:** DRE-Kigali-Game-Builders-Bootcamp Content Campaign  
**Duration:** 4 weeks (February 10 - March 9, 2026)  
**Objective:** Drive awareness, engagement, and registrations for the beginner community program while building DRE's brand as the hub for African game development.  
**Target Audience:** Aspiring game developers, e-sports enthusiasts, tech students in Rwanda/East Africa (ages 18-30)


## Content Pillars
Content will be organized around 4 strategic pillars that align with DRE's mission and program goals:

### Pillar 1: **Education & Empowerment** (40% of content)
**Purpose:** Establish DRE as a learning resource and lower barriers to entry for beginners.

**Content Types:**
- Tutorial snippets (game dev tips, e-sports strategies)
- "Did You Know?" gaming facts with African context
- Beginner-friendly tool recommendations (free software, assets)
- Success stories: "From Beginner to Game Jam Winner"
- Industry insights: African gaming market growth, career paths

**Example Posts:**
- "3 Free Tools Every Beginner Game Dev Needs (No Coding Required!)"
- "Meet Jane: How She Built Her First Game in 48 hours at DRE Game Jam"
- Carousel: "5 Common Game Dev Mistakes Beginners Make (And How to Fix Them)"



### Pillar 2: **Community & Belonging** (30% of content)
**Purpose:** Showcase the vibrant DRE community and make people feel they're joining something special.

**Content Types:**
- Behind-the-scenes of events (setup, candid moments)
- Participant spotlights and testimonials
- User-generated content (repost participant creations with credit)
- Community challenges ("Share your game idea in 3 emojis!")
- Team introductions ("Meet the Mentors")

**Example Posts:**
- Video reel: "A Day in the Life of a DRE Game Jam"
- Quote graphic: "[Participant Name]: 'I never thought I could code. DRE proved me wrong.'"
- Photo carousel: "Week 1 Highlights: 40 Beginners, 1 Incredible Night"


### Pillar 3: **Program Promotion** (20% of content)
**Purpose:** Drive registrations and maintain momentum throughout the 4 weeks.

**Content Types:**
- Event announcements and reminders
- Countdown posts ("3 days until Week 2!")
- Registration CTAs with clear value props
- FAQ posts ("What to bring, what to expect")
- Recap posts with "Join us next week!" hooks

**Example Posts:**
- "Registration is OPEN for Realm Starter Series! 🎮 4 weeks, 0 experience needed. Link in bio."
- "Week 1 was epic. Week 2 is a Game Jam Sprint. will you be there? RSVP now 👉 [link]"
- Graphic: "What You'll Learn in 4 Weeks: Game Dev Basics → Published Game on itch.io"

---

### Pillar 4: **Inspiration & Culture** (10% of content)
**Purpose:** Celebrate African creativity, connect gaming to culture, and inspire pride.

**Content Types:**
- Showcase of African-made games (DRE projects, regional indie games)
- Cultural connections ("How Rwandan folklore could inspire game characters")
- Industry role models (African game devs, e-sports pros)
- Motivational quotes from gaming legends
- Celebration of milestones (GGJ anniversaries, DRE achievements)

**Example Posts:**
- "African stories are the next frontier in gaming. What legends from your culture would make an epic game?"
- Feature post: "5 African Indie Games You Should Play Right Now"
- Video: "Why the World Needs More African Game Developers (ft. DRE Founder)"

## Content Approval Workflow

### Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities | Approval Authority |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Content Creator** | Draft posts, source visuals, write captions | N/A |
| **Community Manager** | Review for brand voice, accuracy, engagement potential | Approve/Request revisions |
| **Marketing Lead** | Final approval for sensitive/strategic content | Final approval (24hr turnaround) |
| **Social Media Manager** | Schedule approved posts, monitor engagement, respond to comments | N/A |

### Workflow Steps

1. **Content Creation** (T-3 days before publish)
   - Content Creator drafts post in shared content bank (Notion)
   - Includes: copy, visuals (mockup or final), platform, CTA, hashtags

2. **First Review** (T-2 days)
   - Community Manager reviews in content bank
   - Flags: "Approved," "Needs Revision," or "Escalate to Marketing Lead"
   - Leaves feedback as comments

3. **Revision** (T-1.5 days, if needed)
   - Content Creator makes edits based on feedback
   - Re-submits for approval

4. **Final Approval** (T-1 day)
   - Marketing Lead approves strategic/sensitive posts (partnerships, announcements)
   - Routine posts auto-approved after Community Manager sign-off

5. **Scheduling** (T-12 hours)
   - Social Media Manager schedules via Buffer, Meta Business Suite, or Hootsuite
   - Double-checks link tracking, hashtags, alt text

6. **Publishing & Monitoring** (Day-of)
   - Post goes live
   - Social Media Manager monitors for first 2 hours: respond to comments, fix errors

7. **Performance Tracking** (Weekly)
   - Track metrics in shared dashboard (impressions, engagement, clicks, conversions)
   - Community Manager flags top/bottom performers for iteration

### Emergency Approval Process
For **time-sensitive posts** (breaking news, last-minute event changes):
- Content Creator tags Community Manager in WhatsApp
- 2-hour approval SLA
- Marketing Lead bypassed if unavailable (Community Manager has authority)

## Content Guidelines (Brand Voice & Style)

### Tone
- **Welcoming:** Use inclusive language ("We're all beginners here," "No dumb questions")
- **Encouraging:** Celebrate small wins, highlight progress over perfection
- **Authentic:** Share real stories, admit challenges, avoid corporate-speak
- **Playful:** Use gaming references, memes, emojis (but don't overdo it)

### Writing Style
- **Keep it concise:** Instagram captions ≤ 150 words, tweets ≤ 280 chars
- **Start with a hook:** First sentence grabs attention ("Want to build a game in 2 weeks? Here's how.")
- **Use clear CTAs:** "Register now," "Join Discord," "Share your thoughts"
- **Avoid jargon:** Define technical terms or use plain language

### Visual Style
- **Colors:** DRE brand palette 
- **Fonts:** Consistent typography 
- **Imagery:** Prioritize authentic photos from events over stock images
- **Video:** Keep under 60 seconds for Instagram/TikTok, 2-3 min for YouTube/Facebook

## Engagement & Community Management

### Response Protocol
- **Response Time SLA:** Within 2 hours during business hours (8 AM - 8 PM)
- **After-Hours:** Next business day (unless urgent)
- **Escalation:** Complaints, technical issues, or requests for refunds → flag to Community Manager immediately

### Comment/DM Strategy
- **Positive Comments:** Like + personalized reply ("Thanks, [Name]! We're excited to have you!")
- **Questions:** Answer thoroughly, provide links to resources
- **Negative Feedback:** Acknowledge, empathize, offer to resolve via DM ("Sorry to hear that! Let's chat privately to fix this.")
- **Spam/Trolls:** Hide comment, block if repeat offender

### Proactive Engagement
- **Daily Tasks:**
  - Like/comment on participant posts tagging DRE
  - Repost user-generated content to Stories (with credit)
  - Engage with 5-10 relevant posts in #GameDev, #RwandaTech communities
- **Weekly Tasks:**
  - Spotlight 1-2 community members (feature in post)
  - Host Discord AMA or Q&A session with DRE team

## Risk Management (Content-Specific)

### Potential Issues & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Low Engagement** | Weak brand visibility, poor registrations | A/B test post formats, adjust posting times, increase interactive content (polls, Q&As) |
| **Negative Comments** | Brand reputation damage | Pre-written response templates, swift moderation, escalate to leadership if serious |
| **Content Delays** | Missed posting windows, inconsistent presence | Build 1-week content buffer, use evergreen posts as backups |
| **Platform Outages** | Can't publish scheduled posts | Cross-post to multiple platforms, have offline backup plan (email, Discord) |
| **Copyright Issues** | Legal trouble from unauthorized assets | Only use licensed-free images/music, credit creators, maintain asset provenance log |
